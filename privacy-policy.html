Privacy Policy for AstroPop

This Privacy Policy describes how AstroPop (Android package com.aistudio.astropop.qvnrtx) collects, uses, stores, and shares user data. We are committed to protecting player privacy and maintaining full transparency.

1. Information We Collect

  • Google Sign-In Mode (Optional Cloud Sync): If you choose to sign in with Google, AstroPop uses Google Credential Manager and Firebase Authentication to authenticate your session. We store your Firebase User ID (userId), Google display name (displayName), and gameplay progression (level stars, high scores, Star Coins, boosters, unlocked cannon skins, claimed quests, recent mission summaries, and audio/haptics settings) in Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Guest Pilot Mode (Offline Local Save): If you play as a Guest without signing in, AstroPop does not collect or transmit personal identifiers to any server. Your game progress is stored strictly on your device using Android SharedPreferences.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate your optional cloud account and prevent unauthorized access to your saved data.
  • Save and synchronize your Saga levels, Endless Panic high score, Daily Nebula streak, Star Coins, boosters, cannon skins, and quests.
  • Remember your audio and tactile feedback preferences.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Service Providers

AstroPop does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. AstroPop contains no third-party advertising networks and no cross-app tracking SDKs. When you use optional Google Sign-In and cloud sync, data is processed solely by:

  • Google Firebase Authentication (session authentication)
  • Google Cloud Firestore (encrypted cloud database storage for game progress)
  • Google Identity / Credential Manager (Google Sign-In authentication)

4. Device Permissions

AstroPop requests only standard INTERNET permission to enable optional Google Sign-In and cloud save synchronization. It does not access location, camera, microphone, contacts, or external media storage.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Requests

Guest players can remove all local data at any time by clearing AstroPop's app storage or uninstalling the app. Signed-in players may sign out from the Settings menu or request permanent deletion of their Firebase account and Firestore game data by contacting us at muhammadjawwad94@gmail.com.

6. Children's Privacy

AstroPop does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age under local law). If you believe a child has provided personal information through Google Sign-In, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

7. Changes & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy as AstroPop evolves. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Questions or requests? Email muhammadjawwad94@gmail.com.
